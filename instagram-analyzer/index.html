Find out who doesn't follow you back — 100% offline, your data never leaves your device.

How to get your data

  1. 1 Open Instagram → Settings → Your activity → Download your information
  2. 2 Select JSON format, choose Followers and Following only, and request the download.
  3. 3 Instagram emails you a link (can take minutes to hours). Download and unzip the file.
  4. 4 Find followers_1.json inside connections/followers_and_following/ and the following.json in the same folder.
  5. 5 Upload both files below.
